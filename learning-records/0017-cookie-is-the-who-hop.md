# Cookie capture is for the “who” hop, not a cookie widget

They asked why the panel needs cookie information. The gap: P1.3 looked like collecting cookies for their own sake. The panel’s job is a stage the URL cannot show — same path, different stored facts (`attach_context`). The Cookie header is the ticket string (already in P1.1 headers); user/session keys are what Django loaded from that ticket. Together they are one type change the Data Panel has to display.
